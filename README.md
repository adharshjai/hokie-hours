<h1 align="center">Hokie Hours</h1>

<p align="center">
  A Chrome extension that shows how many hours you have left under the 20-hour weekly limit
  on your Virginia Tech Banner timesheet. It counts down live while you're clocked in.
</p>

## Install

1. Click the green **Code** button on this page, then **Download ZIP**, and unzip it.
   Keep the folder somewhere permanent, because Chrome runs the extension from it.
2. In Chrome, go to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top-right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the **`hokie-hours-extension`** folder inside the download.
5. Open your timesheet in [Banner](https://selfservice.banner.vt.edu/EmployeeSelfService/ssb/hrDashboard)
   (**Enter Time** → your pay period). The hours pill appears at the top of the page.

## Update

Replace the files in the same folder, click the reload arrow on Hokie Hours in `chrome://extensions`,
then refresh Banner.

## Notes

- Read-only. It can't clock you in or out, edit, save, or submit anything.
- Your hours are stored only on your computer.
- Unofficial and not affiliated with Virginia Tech. Banner is the official record.
